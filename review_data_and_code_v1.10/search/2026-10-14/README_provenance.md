# Search records retained from 14 October 2026

These records document the targeted lookup of 14 October 2026. They were compiled on 15 October 2026 from the archived responses; the queries were not rerun.

## Contents and counts

`query_index.csv` records 57 operations: 30 search queries, 23 page opens, 3 link clicks and one combined publisher and Crossref identity check. `raw_record` names the archived response batch of each operation.

`retrieval_outcomes.csv` records 18 full-text retrieval attempts. Eight downloads succeeded, 3 PDF retrievals failed and 7 attempts did not identify an open-access route. A download can succeed after an earlier failed page open of the same URL; attempts are listed separately and are not merged into one result. `file_reference` gives the archive-relative location of each downloaded PDF.

`negative_outcomes.csv` records 20 page access failures and unsuccessful full-text or open-access retrieval attempts. They are not zero-result literature searches. The web responses mix hits from multi-query batches, so per-query result totals and zero-hit status are not reported.

Supplement fields that read `not_found` because a direct PDF URL did not identify a landing page describe a limitation of the retrieval route. They do not show that a publication has no supplement.

## Scope and rights

The queries focus on named classical references, title checks, versions and full-text access. They support a targeted supplementary lookup, not a comprehensive search by mechanism and learning method. No source-study PDFs are included.

Raw response files are not included because they contain third-party search snippets and retrieved web-page text. Paths beginning with `raw/` identify archived responses, not downloadable files in this deposit. The factual indexes and disposition records are licensed as part of the review data. Third-party quoted content keeps its original rights. The interpretation fields record AI-assisted checks made on 15 October 2026 (article Section 2.2).
