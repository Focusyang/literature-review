# Search records retained from 14 October 2026

These records were recovered on 15 October 2026 from the author's local archive of version 1.5. The queries were not rerun, and missing historical outcomes were not reconstructed.

## Contents and counts

The original index records 59 operations: 30 search queries, 25 page opens, 3 link clicks and one combined publisher and Crossref identity check. Sixteen web-tool response files and the cited Crossref identity response are retained byte-for-byte in the author's local archive, outside this public package. The normalized index (`query_index.csv`) changes only raw-record paths and adds interpretation fields. `query_index_original.csv` preserves the historical index bytes.

`retrieval_outcomes.csv` normalizes the archived retrieval index into 18 attempts. Eight downloads succeeded, 3 PDF retrievals failed and 7 attempts did not identify an open-access route. The historical index includes eight successful retrievals even when an earlier web-tool open of the same URL failed. Different tools and attempts are not merged into one result.

`negative_outcomes.csv` records page access failures and unsuccessful full-text or open-access retrieval attempts. They are not zero-result literature searches. No query-level zero-hit record was found. The web responses mix hits from multi-query batches, so an absent identifiable hit for an individual query does not establish zero results. Query result totals, unreturned results and a complete census of negative searches cannot be reconstructed.

Supplement fields that read `not_found` because a direct PDF URL did not identify a landing page describe a limitation of the retrieval route. They do not show that a publication has no supplement.

## Scope and preservation

The retained queries focus on named classical references, title checks, versions and full-text access. They support a targeted supplementary lookup, not a new comprehensive search by mechanism and learning method. No paired-query protocol for every named mechanism was retained from that time. The original web batches also include journal-policy lookups, so 59 is an operation count, not a literature-search or study count.

`provenance_manifest.csv` maps every locally retained raw file to its original relative workspace location and SHA-256. Historical absolute paths in retrieval records were converted to workspace-relative references in the normalized table. No source-study PDFs are included.

Raw response files are not included because they contain third-party search snippets and retrieved web-page text. Paths beginning with `raw/` in the indexes identify locally retained historical responses, not downloadable files in this deposit. A dataset license does not transfer their copyright. The factual indexes and disposition records are licensed as part of the review data. Third-party quoted content keeps its original rights.

The interpretation fields describe AI-assisted checks made on 15 October 2026. They are not independent human verification.
