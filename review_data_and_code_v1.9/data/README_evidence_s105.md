# S105 source evidence

`evidence_s105.csv` gives page locators for the eight coded variants of S105 (Sadykov et al., 2019, diving heuristics for branch-and-price). Five further rows locate numerical and interpretive statements. The table was prepared on 15 October 2026 from the 37-page HAL v3 author manuscript (hal-01237204v3). The HAL cover gives the submission date as 13 April 2017 and links the 2019 journal article. The article cites the journal version and uses this author manuscript for page locators. Every row records the SHA-256 of the PDF read.

PDF page 1 is the HAL cover. Printed manuscript page numbers are one less than the 1-based PDF page numbers. The journal pagination (251-267) does not apply to these locators.

In version 1.9 the scope entry of S105-M7 reads E2, matching the coded inventory. S105-M7 solves an integer restricted master over the columns generated at the root, without further column generation. The other entries are unchanged from version 1.7.

The rows record AI-assisted source checks. They are not independent human coding and do not report new solver results.
